¡Hola! I'm Aaron Roman, Co-Founder and Chief Data Scientist at [Danelfin](https://danelfin.com), where we're revolutionizing investment analysis through artificial intelligence.

## My Journey: From Code to Capital Markets

As Chief Data Scientist at Danelfin, I've had the privilege of co-founding a platform that's changing how investors approach the stock market. Our mission is simple yet powerful: **democratize AI-powered investment analysis**.

For too long, sophisticated AI technology was exclusively available to hedge funds and elite investors. Together with my co-founder Tomás Diago (former Softonic founder), we decided it was time to change that and bring analytical power to all investors worldwide.

## What We're Building at Danelfin

At Danelfin, we've developed an AI-based stock and ETF analysis platform that:

- **Rates investments** on a 1-10 scale
- **Predicts market performance** for US and European stocks
- **Makes complex analysis accessible** to retail investors
- **Leverages cutting-edge ML models** for financial insights

We recently closed a €2 million funding round led by Nauta Capital, which validates our vision of making advanced investment tools available to everyone.
